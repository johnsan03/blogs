The kettle sings in silver steam,  
and windows blush with dawn.  
I gather yesterday’s loose threads,  
then watch them come undone.

If I could name this quiet thing,  
I’d call it **starting over**—  
not loud, not perfect, not complete,  
just light on the floor.
